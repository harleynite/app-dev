## Overview

**Inception** is a mind-bending science fiction thriller directed by **Christopher Nolan**. The film explores the concept of dream infiltration and manipulation through cutting-edge technology.

---

## Cast

- **Leonardo DiCaprio** as Dom Cobb
- **Joseph Gordon-Levitt** as Arthur
- **Elliot Page** as Ariadne
- **Tom Hardy** as Eames
- **Ken Watanabe** as Saito
- **Marion Cotillard** as Mal

---

## Plot Summary

Dom Cobb is a skilled thief who specializes in stealing secrets from people's subconscious while they dream. He's offered a chance at redemption: instead of stealing an idea, he must *plant* one deep within a target's mind—a process called **inception**.

> *"You mustn't be afraid to dream a little bigger, darling." - Eames*

---

## Key Themes

1. Reality vs. Dreams
2. Guilt and Redemption
3. The Power of Ideas
4. Memory and Loss
